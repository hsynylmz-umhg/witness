Five verbs, exported by motion.ts and nothing else: reveal (a mask edge uncovers content already in place), drawOn (a stroke grows along its length), typeOn (words appear character by character within 15 frames), step (a state changes once; a stated number lands whole in one frame; type changes size only between two of the theme's five sizes), rule (a hairline is drawn, or carried to a new extent).
Nothing enters from outside the frame, no image scales, nothing counts up, and the camera never moves; content does not translate while it is revealed.
Two easings only, entrance and state, both in theme.ts; nothing except a draw-on runs longer than 20 frames, and a spring never oscillates.
The spine is one rule at one y from the first frame to the last, drawn once; at each boundary only its weight and colour step to the incoming role: underline, log header, chain baseline, figure baseline, section rule, axis, crop line, closing rule.
Continuity: a beat's first object arrives LEAD frames before its boundary and its last object stays LINGER frames past the next one; nothing arrives during beat 4's hold or beat 6's freeze, where the spine is the incoming object. Carried objects: the still's frame becomes the report's frame (2 to 3), the cause outline opens into the chart frame (5 to 6), the chart frame becomes the overlay crop (6 to 7).
Banned: fades or cross-dissolves between beats, slide-ins, fly-ins, zooms, push-ins, spins, bounces, counters, decorative gradients, shadows on type, glowing outlines, particles, node backdrops, lens flares, blue-on-blue glow.
Every colour, size, radius, easing and duration comes from theme.ts; no literal colour or pixel unit appears anywhere else under src.
To add a beat: author it in beats.ts (its slot is its clip plus 2.000 s, rounded up to a frame, and the total is asserted), compose it from the five verbs, carry an object across both of its boundaries, and run npm run script-doc.
