\## Known defects in the footage — read before interpreting any result



1\. \*\*The print came out rotated 90°.\*\* The sheet prints as 5 columns × 2 rows with the digits

&#x20;  lying on their side, not the 2 × 5 portrait layout the brief specified. Whether this matters

&#x20;  depends on whether the detector reads digits or only tracks motion — that question is part

&#x20;  of what round 5 measures, not something already known.

2\. \*\*The camera angle is roughly 20–25°, not the requested 45–60°.\*\* The near row is

&#x20;  substantially larger than the far row. A motion-threshold detector can catch one row and

&#x20;  miss the other purely because of scale, so a miss on the far row is \*\*not\*\* automatically a

&#x20;  detector failure.

3\. \*\*A hand enters the frame.\*\* Only the pencil tip and fingertips; no box is covered at any

&#x20;  point. Accepted, not a defect, recorded so that a later reviewer does not read it as one.

4\. \*\*The action order cannot be verified from still frames.\*\* The operator reports the

&#x20;  sequences above. Two review stills taken near the end show pencil positions that do not by

&#x20;  themselves confirm either sequence. The written key is the authority; the stills are

&#x20;  not counter-evidence.

5\. \*\*No second take.\*\* This is the only footage that will exist. Round 5 does not get to ask

&#x20;  for a cleaner clip.

\## What this measurement can and cannot claim



It \*\*can\*\* say: the pipeline opened a file it did not generate, and produced (or did not

produce) findings on a scene whose label was fixed in advance.



It \*\*cannot\*\* say: the detector works on real footage in general. Two clips, one scene, one

lighting condition, one camera, one operator. Anything written about this in `STATUS.md` or the

submission has to stay inside that sentence.



A negative result — no findings on the clean clip, no findings on the faulty one — is still a

result and gets recorded as measured. It is not a reason to change the numbers, and it is not a

reason to hide the section.
